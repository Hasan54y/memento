Delete your account.

You can delete your Memento account and data at any time. Here's how, and what happens to your data.

Delete it in the app

The fastest way. It takes effect immediately.

account_circle

Open Profile

Open Memento and tap Profile in the bottom bar.

manage_accounts

Account settings

Tap Account settings and scroll to “Danger zone”.

delete

Delete account

Tap Delete account and confirm. You may be asked to sign in again first.

Can't open the app?

Email us from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm when it's done.

mailRequest deletion

What gets deleted

person_off

Your account

Your sign-in (name, email and password) is permanently removed from our authentication service.

description

Data on your phone

Deleting in the app also erases every document, page photo and note Memento stored on that phone.

add_to_drive

Your Drive backup

Backups live in your own Google Drive, so we can't reach them. Delete “Memento backup.zip” from Drive whenever you like.

We don't keep copies of your documents on our servers. See the Privacy Policy for details.
