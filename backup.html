add_to_drive Google Drive backup

Your memory, safe in your Drive.

Phones get lost, broken and replaced. Your documents shouldn't go with them.

Private by design

It lives in your Drive — not ours.

Memento saves one backup file, “Memento backup.zip”, to your own Google Drive. It uses Google's most limited Drive permission, so Memento can only see files it created — never the rest of your Drive.

  • No Memento server stores your documents
  • Back up or restore any time with one tap
  • Optional automatic backup overnight on Wi-Fi

What's in a backup

description

Documents & details

Every document with its category, title, extracted details, dates and reminders.

image

Page photos

All the scanned pages, so documents look exactly the same after a restore.

edit_note

Notes

Your notes with their formatting, colors and pins.

settings_backup_restore

Restore

Good to know

wifi

Automatic backups

Turn on daily backups and Memento runs them overnight on Wi-Fi after your first backup.

visibility_off

Limited access

Memento only sees the backup file it created — not your other files.

delete

Delete it any time

The backup is a normal file in your Drive. Delete it whenever you like.

key

Revoke access

Remove Memento's Drive access at any time from your Google Account settings.
