How it works

Capture

Tap the + button and scan a document or import photos.

Let AI read it

Memento fills in the category, title, details and important dates. Review and save.

Relax

Search anytime, get reminded before deadlines, and keep it all backed up.

Your data stays yours.

Documents are stored on your phone. Photos are sent to Google's Gemini AI only to read them for you. Backups go to your own Google Drive, and Memento can only see the backup files it creates.

Questions

Is Memento free?

Yes. Memento is free to download and use, with no ads.

Do I need an account?

You sign in inside the app with email or Google so your documents stay tied to you and can be backed up and restored. This website doesn't need any sign-in.

Where are my documents stored?

On your phone, in Memento's private storage. If you turn on backup, a copy goes to your own Google Drive.

How accurate is the AI?

Very good for clear photos, but always double-check important numbers and dates against the original. You can edit anything it fills in.

When can I get it?

Memento is coming to Google Play soon. Tap “Get early access” or email admin@mementoapp.online and we'll let you know as soon as it's available. Once installed, the app checks this website for updates and tells you when a new version is ready.

How do I contact you?

Email admin@mementoapp.online. You can also send feedback from Profile → Send feedback in the app.
