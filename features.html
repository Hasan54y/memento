auto_awesome Features

Everything your paperwork needs.

Memento turns photos of documents into organized, searchable information — then keeps an eye on the dates for you.

Capture + AI

Snap it. AI fills in the rest.

Scan with the built-in camera or import from your gallery. Memento's AI reads every page together and fills in the category, title, details and the date that matters.

  • Multi-page documents — add as many pages as you need
  • Import up to 10 photos at once
  • Suggests an important date and a reminder
  • Tap “Read again” if a photo was blurry — or edit anything yourself
Documents

Every detail at a glance.

Each document gets a clean page: swipe through its pages, zoom in, copy any field with a tap, star favorites, edit, share or delete.

  • Full-screen zoom with pinch and double-tap
  • Tap to copy numbers, addresses and IDs
  • Countdown to the important date
Search

Find anything in seconds.

Search reads titles, every extracted detail and your notes. Filter by category to narrow it down instantly.

  • Search documents and notes together
  • Quick filters by category
  • Suggestions to get you started
Save as files

PDF, Excel, TXT or images.

Need a copy for an insurance claim or your accountant? Save any document to your phone in the format that fits.

  • PDF with a summary page plus every scanned page
  • Excel spreadsheet (.xlsx) of all the details
  • Plain text you can open anywhere
  • Page images straight to your Gallery
  • Export all documents as one spreadsheet
Google Drive backup

Backed up to your own Drive.

Keep a private backup in your Google Drive and restore everything on a new phone. Memento can only see the backup file it creates.

  • One tap to back up or restore
  • Optional automatic backup overnight on Wi-Fi
How backup works
Design

Beautiful day and night.

A clean, modern look with glass surfaces and smooth animations. Follows your phone's theme, or pick light or dark yourself.

  • Dark, light or system theme
  • Full-screen, edge-to-edge design
  • Choose a preset avatar for your profile
