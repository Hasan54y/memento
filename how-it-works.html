tips_and_updates How it works

From photo to organized in seconds.

No typing, no folders, no spreadsheets. Here's what happens when you add something to Memento.

login

Sign in

Create an account with email or continue with Google. Your documents stay tied to you.

add_a_photo

Capture

Tap +, then scan with the camera, import photos, or write a note.

auto_awesome

AI reads it

The details are filled in for you. Check them, change anything, and save.

notifications_active

Stay ahead

Search anytime, get reminded before dates, and keep it backed up.

The AI part

Watch it read the page.

Memento sends your page photos to Google's Gemini AI through Firebase, which reads all the pages together — like a person skimming the document — and returns the details in a structured form.

  • Picks the right category automatically
  • Pulls out store, product, price, numbers and dates
  • Suggests the date worth a reminder
  • Everything stays editable — you're always in control

Tips for the best results

Clear photos mean better answers.

wb_sunny

Good light

Daylight or a bright room. Avoid harsh shadows and glare on glossy paper.

crop_free

Whole page in frame

Keep all four corners visible so nothing important is cut off.

straighten

Flat and steady

Lay the document flat and hold the phone parallel to it.

auto_stories

One item, many pages

Add every page of the same document to one item — AI reads them together.

refresh

Read again

Blurry result? Retake the photo and tap “Read again”.

fact_check

Double-check key numbers

AI is very good, but always compare important numbers and dates with the original.

What happens to your photos?

They're stored in Memento's private storage on your phone. They're sent to Gemini only to read them for you — never for advertising, and never to train models.
